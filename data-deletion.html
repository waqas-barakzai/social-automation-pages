How to delete data stored by the App

Social Automation Center is designed primarily as a local desktop application. To remove local application data:

  1. Stop the Social Automation Center service/application.
  2. Remove the application's local data directory, including its database, logs, cached media, and locally stored connection data.
  3. Delete any content folders or backups you no longer need.

How to revoke social-media access

Removing local application data does not automatically revoke authorization held by a social-media platform. To revoke access, open the relevant platform's account/business settings and remove Social Automation Center (or the associated developer application) from the list of authorized apps.

Scope
