1. Who we are

Social Automation Center ("the App", "we", "us") is a desktop application that helps users organize content, connect authorized social-media accounts, prepare platform-specific posts, schedule publishing, and track publishing activity.

2. Information the App processes

Depending on the features you enable, the App may process:

  • Account connection information and platform identifiers returned by social-media authorization flows.
  • Access and refresh tokens required to maintain authorized connections.
  • Page, channel, profile, and destination names/IDs.
  • Local media files, filenames, metadata, captions, titles, hashtags, scheduling information, and publishing history.
  • Optional AI prompts and generated content when you enable an AI provider.
  • Technical logs needed to diagnose errors and maintain reliable publishing jobs.

3. How information is used

  • To connect to and operate the social accounts you explicitly authorize.
  • To ingest, process, transform, queue, schedule, and publish content you instruct the App to publish.
  • To monitor job status, handle retries, and maintain activity records.
  • To provide support and troubleshoot technical problems.

4. Local processing and third-party services

The App is designed as an always-on local desktop application. Where the feature is implemented locally, media and configuration data can remain on your PC. When you enable a third-party service such as a social-media platform or an optional AI provider, the App sends only the information necessary for that enabled feature.

5. Social-media platforms

When you connect Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Snapchat, or another platform, that platform may process your information under its own privacy policy and terms. We do not control the privacy practices of those platforms. You should review their current terms before authorizing the App.

6. Security

We aim to protect stored credentials and configuration using appropriate local security controls. You are responsible for securing the Windows user account, PC, backups, and any credentials used with the App.

7. Data retention and deletion

You control the content folders used by the App. When automatic cleanup is enabled, the App can delete local media after successful publication according to your configured rules. Connection data can be removed from the App and you can revoke the App's authorization from the relevant social-media platform.

8. Children's privacy

The App is intended for adults and businesses. It is not designed for children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy when the App's functionality or legal requirements change. The "Last updated" date will be revised when material changes are made.
